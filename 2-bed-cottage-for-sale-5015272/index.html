Gordon Hughes Estate Agents are delighted to present to the market this charming detached former schoolhouse dating back to circa 1840s, occupying a truly unique lakeside setting overlooking Brackley Lake in Bawnboy, Co. Cavan. Situated on a generous site this 2 bedroom property extending to approximately 0.33 hectares (0.82 acres) the property enjoys elevated lakeside views & is only a short drive from the village of Bawnboy & the nearby towns of Ballinamore & Ballyconnell.

Full of character and charm throughout, this distinctive property offers cosy accommodation comprising an open plan kitchen/living/dining area featuring a traditional solid fuel stove set within a stone surround, shower room, bedroom and a mezzanine level with sitting area and further bedroom with WC.

The property benefits from Oil Fired Central Heating (OFCH), private bored well water supply and enjoys stunning views over Brackley Lake and the surrounding countryside.

Offering a rare opportunity to acquire a property of immense character in a peaceful rural setting, this former schoolhouse would make an ideal permanent residence, holiday home or countryside retreat while enjoying the tranquillity of this beautiful lakeside location.
