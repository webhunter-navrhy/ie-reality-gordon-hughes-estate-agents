About the property

Description property

Gordon Hughes Estate Agents are delighted to present 60 Jamestown Park to the market, an attractive four bedroomed semi-detached home ideally positioned in the ever-popular Jamestown Park development in Ratoath.

Extending to approx. 123sqm, this well-proportioned home offers generous accommodation and excellent potential for modern family living. The property benefits from an attractive Tudor-style façade, with the front elevation complemented by mature planting and a spacious cobble lock driveway providing ample off-street parking.

On entering, the property offers a welcoming sense of space, with well-balanced accommodation throughout and excellent potential for the new owners to put their own stamp on the home. The layout is ideally suited to family life, with four bedrooms and three bathrooms providing comfortable accommodation for growing families, first time buyers or these looking to trade up. To the rear the property enjoys a private and mature rear garden, which is predominantly laid in lawn.

Jamestown Park is a highly regarded residential development, with Ratoath offering an excellent choice of schools, shops, sporting facilities, restaurants and everyday amenities. The development is well positioned for commuters, making this an attractive location for those seeking a balance between 'village' living and accessibility to Dublin.

Viewing is highly recommended!
